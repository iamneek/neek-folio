I will be trying to use these principles as a set of guiding principles for my life. Currently, I am far from what I aspire to be, but with time I will be there. The reason for posting this publicly instead of internalizing this is so that this becomes a form of public commitment which I can't run away from.

I have thought about some of them, some of them, I've borrowed, some were inspired by my great heroes. These principles, I hope to keep adding and updating throughout my life, as I progress in life.

### THE PRINCIPLES

1. It's one life and the world is a playground, the purpose of my life is to leave this playground after building something great for the future generations to play with, i.e. build something for the world as a gift to the world that has given me a chance to live and experience stuffs. So the biggest principle of all is to follow my purpose with passion and with highest priority of all, and build myself towards fulfilling it.
2. I will never stop learning till my brain is functional and can consume information.
3. If I face a setback and if the thing is important, and is something I care about, I will retry doing things for *at least* 4 times before giving up or in some exceptions > 4 times.
4. I will remain optimistic until the end results are out.
5. I will never be afraid to ask questions, no matter how absurd.
6. I will say 'YES' to things I truly want to do, and a 'NO' with genuine reason whenever I feel like I should say a NO.
7. I will befriend people who have ambitious goals, and are working towards it, and in their surrounding, I'll grow.
8. I will consistently exercise both my mind and my body.
9. I will mostly be playful, because life should only be serious if it's a seriously sad occasion, or if it's related to my goal and my purpose.
10. I can just do things.
